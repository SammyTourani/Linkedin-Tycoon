# 🚀 Quick Start Guide - LinkedIn Tycoon Pixel Edition

## ⚡ Play Now (3 Easy Steps)

### Option 1: Direct Browser (Simplest)
1. Double-click `pixel-game.html` in your file browser
2. Use WASD/Arrows to move, E to interact
3. Start building your empire!

### Option 2: Local Server (Recommended)
1. Open Terminal in this folder
2. Run: `python3 -m http.server 8888`
3. Visit: http://localhost:8888/pixel-game.html

---

## 🎮 Controls

| Key | Action |
|-----|--------|
| **WASD** or **Arrows** | Move character |
| **E** | Interact with objects/people |
| **Mouse** | Click menu buttons |
| **ESC** | Close menus |

---

## 🏠 What to Do First

### In Your Home:
1. Walk to the **💻 Computer** (press E)
2. Click **"Create Post"** to earn your first coins and XP
3. Walk to the **🚪 Door** at bottom (press E) to go outside

### In the City:
1. Walk around and find **👥 People** 
2. Press **E** near them to network (costs 10 energy)
3. Visit the **🎯 Conference Center** (blue building)
4. Press **E** to attend event (costs 20 energy, earn 100 coins!)
5. Visit **☕ Coffee Shop** (brown building) when low on energy

---

## 💡 Quick Tips

- **Energy regenerates** +1 every 10 seconds
- **Sleep in bed** to restore 50 energy instantly
- **Network with all 5 people** in the city for maximum XP
- **Create posts often** - they can go viral for huge rewards!
- **Attend events** when you have 20+ energy for best returns
- **Game auto-saves** every 30 seconds - progress is safe!
