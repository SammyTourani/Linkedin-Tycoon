# 🔷 LinkedIn Tycoon

**Build Your Professional Empire!**

LinkedIn Tycoon is an immersive incremental tycoon game where you become a rising LinkedIn professional, building the ultimate career network and influencer brand. Manage your connections, create viral posts, attend virtual events, complete challenges, and balance networking with personal growth.

## 🎮 Game Features

### 1. Player Profile & Progression
- Start as a Junior Software Engineer with core stats: Networking, Skills, Reputation, and Energy
- Level up by gaining connections, endorsements, job offers, and followers
- Unlock badges and achievements as you progress
- Upgrade your profile picture, background, and headline
- Track your progress with detailed metrics

### 2. Networking System
- Send personalized connection requests with AI-generated messages
- AI-powered matchmaking based on interests, skills, and levels
- Connection requests can be accepted or rejected with realistic simulation
- Auto-follow recommended profiles for passive growth
- Manage pending requests and view mutual connections

### 3. Content Creation & Virality
- Create LinkedIn posts with multiple content types:
  - Status Updates
  - Articles
  - Videos
  - Polls
- AI-generated text suggestions and hashtag recommendations
- Realistic engagement simulation (likes, comments, shares, views)
- Post performance driven by timing, quality, and player stats
- Viral posts boost networking rate and follower gain

### 4. Virtual Events & Challenges
- Attend AI-simulated virtual conferences and webinars
- Participate in hackathons with unique challenges
- Complete networking mixers and career workshops
- Earn exclusive rewards including XP, coins, reputation, and skill boosts
- Unlock special badges for event participation

### 5. Energy & Time Management
- Limited energy system for strategic gameplay
- Energy regenerates automatically over time (1 point per 10 seconds)
- Prioritize between networking, content creation, skill upgrading, and rest
- Purchase energy drinks from the shop for instant refills

### 6. Skill Tree & Reputation
- Three skill categories to develop:
  - **Technical Skills**: JavaScript, Python, Cloud Computing, AI/ML
  - **Soft Skills**: Leadership, Communication, Teamwork, Problem Solving
  - **Marketing Skills**: Content Marketing, SEO, Social Media, Analytics
- Each skill has 10 levels to master
- Reputation affects networking success and job offers
- Upgrade skills via courses and investments

### 7. Monetization Simulator
- Earn virtual currency from various activities
- Purchase profile boosts and sponsored posts
- Invest in premium features and energy drinks
- Manage your virtual career budget strategically

### 8. Social Leaderboards & Achievements
- Compete on weekly leaderboards for top networker status
- Unlock achievements like "Super Connector" and "Viral Content Creator"
- Earn exclusive badges for milestones
- Track your progress against AI competitors

## 🚀 How to Play

### Getting Started
1. Open `index.html` in your web browser
2. The game auto-saves every 30 seconds to localStorage
3. Your progress is preserved between sessions

### Basic Actions

#### Networking (🤝 Network Tab)
- Click "Find New Connections" to discover new professionals (costs 10 energy)
- Click "Connect" on suggested profiles to send a connection request
- Write personalized messages to increase acceptance rate
- Use "Auto-Connect" for quick networking (costs 50 coins)

#### Creating Content (✍️ Create Post Tab)
1. Choose a post type (Update, Article, Video, or Poll)
2. Write your content or click "Generate Idea" for AI suggestions
3. Click "Add Hashtags" to boost engagement
4. Publish your post (costs 15 energy)
5. Watch engagement grow in real-time

#### Attending Events (🎯 Events Tab)
- Browse available virtual events
- Check energy costs and rewards
- Attend events to gain XP, coins, and stat boosts
- Complete challenges for exclusive badges

#### Developing Skills (📚 Skills Tab)
- View your skill tree across three categories
- Upgrade skills using coins
- Higher skill levels unlock better opportunities
- Master skills to earn special badges

### Tips for Success
1. **Manage Your Energy**: Don't spend all your energy at once. Plan your actions strategically.
2. **Post Regularly**: Consistent posting builds followers and reputation faster.
3. **Use Hashtags**: Posts with hashtags get better engagement.
4. **Network Actively**: More connections lead to more opportunities.
5. **Attend Events**: Events provide the best rewards for progression.
6. **Upgrade Skills**: Invest in diverse skills for balanced growth.
7. **Watch for Notifications**: Random events can provide bonuses!

## 🔄 Game Mechanics

### Energy Regeneration
- Energy regenerates at 1 point per 10 seconds
- Maximum energy increases with level-ups and premium features
- Actions cost between 10-30 energy depending on importance

### Post Engagement Formula
Post engagement is calculated based on:
- Number of followers (base audience)
- Hashtag count (bonus engagement)
- Reputation score (multiplier)
- Post type (different types have different viral potential)
- Random virality chance

### Connection Acceptance Rate
Connection request acceptance is influenced by:
- Your networking stat (higher = better chance)
- Your reputation (higher = better chance)
- Message quality (longer, personalized messages help)
- Base 50% acceptance rate with bonuses

### XP and Leveling
- XP required for next level increases by 1.5x each level
- Earn XP from:
  - Making connections: 15 XP
  - Publishing posts: 20-100 XP
  - Attending events: 30-100 XP
  - Upgrading skills: 25 XP
  - Completing achievements: 50 XP

## 📝 Tips for Developers

Want to modify the game? Here's a quick guide:

### Adding New Events
Edit the `generateEvents()` method in `game.js`:
```javascript
{
    name: 'Your Event Name',
    type: 'event_type',
    description: 'Event description',
    duration: 60, // minutes
    reward: { xp: 50, coins: 100 },
    cost: 15 // energy cost
}
```

### Adding New Skills
Edit the `createSkillTree()` method in `game.js`:
```javascript
{ 
    id: 'skill_id', 
    name: 'Skill Name', 
    level: 0, 
    maxLevel: 10, 
    cost: 50 
}
```

### Customizing Player Stats
Edit the `createNewGame()` method to change starting stats.

### Styling Changes
Modify CSS variables in `styles.css`:
```css
:root {
    --primary-color: #0a66c2;
    --success-color: #057642;
    /* etc. */
}
```
