Meet **5 unique professionals** with distinct personalities:

- Pragmatic and direct
- Advises on technical career growth

#### **Emily Rodriguez**
- Tech Recruiter

- Shares recruiting/job search wisdom

#### **David Park**
- YouTube Tech Content Creator
- Casual and energetic
- Reveals content creation secrets

#### **Dr. Jennifer Liu**
- Computer Science Professor & Mentor
- Wise and measured
- Focuses on long-term career strategy

**How Dialogue Works:**
1. Walk up to any NPC and press **E**
2. Read their introduction
3. Choose from 3 dialogue options:
   - 💼 "Tell me about networking"
   - 💡 "Got any advice?"
   - 👋 "Nice meeting you!" (Ends dialogue & connects)
4. Each NPC has unique responses based on their personality
5. Connecting after dialogue = 80% success rate (vs 70% without)
